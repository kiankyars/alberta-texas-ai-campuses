## TX07 — Adopted large-load interconnection standard, 16 TAC 25.194

Publisher: Texas Secretary of State / Public Utility Commission of Texas. Published: 2026-10-02; effective 2026-10-08. [Source](https://www.sos.state.tx.us/texreg/pdf/backview/1002/1002is.pdf)

Supported: Adopted text at printed pages 6931-6940 covers 75 MW+ loads; $100,000 study fee, $50,000/MW intermediate security, and final security at least the greater of that basis or allocated upgrade costs.

Limit: Counsel must apply full rule, tariff, disclosures, milestones and credit/forfeiture provisions. Securities are not a construction-price quotation.
